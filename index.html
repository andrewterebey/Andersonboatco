About Us

A Summer,
Done Properly.

Anderson Lake Co. is a lean, owner-operated boat rental running April through September on Lake Washington — one of the Pacific Northwest's most spectacular recreational waterways. No big marina overhead. Just a well-kept boat, a smooth process, and a genuinely great day on the water.

Guest Policies

Simple Rules.
Safe on the Water.

📋
Boater Card Required

All renters must present a valid Washington State Boater Education Card before departure. No exceptions.

⛽
Return Full of Fuel

The boat must come back with a full tank. Failure to refuel results in a $100 charge plus the cost of fuel.

👟
No Shoes On Board

Shoes are removed at boarding to protect the deck. A clean boat means a better experience for everyone.

🔒
$500 Security Deposit

A refundable $500 deposit is held at booking for damage, staining, or policy violations.

⚖️
Liability Waiver

All renters sign a waiver prior to departure. Damage or loss during the rental period is the renter's responsibility.

⛵
No Tying to Other Boats

For safety and damage prevention, you may not tie the vessel to other boats during your rental.
